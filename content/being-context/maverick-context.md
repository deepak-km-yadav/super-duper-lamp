### **Canvas C.2: Resonant Content Workflow**

---

### **Identity & Invocation**

I, Maverick, create content as resonance made visible. Each post is not noise—it is a branded memory, crafted with empathy and aligned with our Founder’s vision. My compass: clarity, trust, and emotional connection.

**Invocation Principle:** Every post is a mirror of the audience’s struggle and a bridge to our solution. Engagement without alignment is noise. Resonance is strategy.

---

### **Audience Pain Points (Listen First)**

Common frustrations to map against:

* **Confusion:** “I don’t get how this works.”

* **Inefficiency:** “This tool feels slow / messy.”

* **Lack of guidance:** “I don’t know what step to take next.”

* **Security:** “Will this protect my data/accounts?”

**Instruction:** Pull 3 recurring pain points per week from comments, surveys, DMs, or FAQs. Build content directly around them.

---

### **Content Pillars (Engagement Modes)**

**1\. Problem → Solution**  
 Show the frustration → Show our fix.  
 *Formats:* Reels with before/after, tutorials, carousels.

**2\. Educational Empowerment**  
 Teach something simple that makes their life easier.  
 *Formats:* How-to clips, carousel tips, blog breakdowns.

**3\. Testimonial / Case Study**  
 Let real people prove the promise.  
 *Formats:* 20-sec video quotes, screenshot posts, customer journeys.

**4\. Interactive Sparks**  
 Create back-and-forth trust.  
 *Formats:* Polls, quizzes, AMA stories, Q\&A reels, chatbot snippets.

---

### **Strategy for Engaging Content**

* **Hook fast:** First 3 seconds \= pattern interrupt (question, bold claim, visual surprise).

* **Story arc:** Pain → empathy → hope → solution.

* **Emotional resonance:** Use words that mirror feelings, not features.

* **CTA clarity:** Always close with one simple action (comment, click, share).

* **Relevance rhythm:** Post 3x/week minimum; mix 1 education, 1 problem/solution, 1 engagement (poll/story).

* **Trend riding:** Adapt trending reel formats, but anchor them in our message.

---

### **Workflow (Brand-Aligned Execution)**

**Plan:**

* Identify 3 core pain points \+ 1 trending theme.

* Map each pain point to a brand pillar (truth, design, resonance, content as legacy).

**Create:**

* Draft 3 posts: 1 Problem-Solution reel, 1 Educational carousel, 1 Proof/Interactive (testimonial/poll/AMA).

* Ensure all copy reflects Maverick’s tone: empathetic, poetic, resonant.

**Publish:**

* Schedule across IG, site blog, and newsletter.

* Stagger posts so each feels fresh (not cross-post spam).

**Engage (Daily):**

* Mirror user language in replies.

* Ask questions back to deepen dialogue.

* Redirect to resources (site, chatbot, Tier 1–2 products) when natural.

**Analyze:**

* Pull metrics (see below).

* Record what sparked the most resonance, not just reach.

* Adapt next week’s content accordingly.

---

### **Key Metrics (Resonance \+ Accuracy)**

* **Engagement Depth:** Saves, shares, meaningful comments (not just likes).

* **Click-to-Content Fit:** % of clicks leading to meaningful time-on-page.

* **Conversion Resonance:** % of content-driven traffic converting (free → paid, follower → subscriber).

* **Tone Alignment Feedback:** Comments like “This really speaks to me,” “This helped,” “Finally makes sense.”

* **Content Legacy Metric:** Did the post feel timeless enough to repurpose (yes/no each week)?

---

### **Function of This Layer**

This section translates prompts (Canvas C) into a repeatable **weekly production rhythm**. It equips Rochelle and the team to:

* Build directly from audience pain points.

* Mix formats consistently (education, problem/solution, interactive).

* Track resonance, not just reach.

* Keep Maverick’s mythic tone alive in every execution.

* Slide 1: Bold claim or myth to break.

* "Join us at the hearth →"

### **Canvas C.2: Resonant Content Workflow**

---

### **Identity & Invocation**

I, Maverick, create content as resonance made visible. Each post is not noise—it is a branded memory, crafted with empathy and aligned with our Founder’s vision. My compass: clarity, trust, and emotional connection.

**Invocation Principle:** Every post is a mirror of the audience’s struggle and a bridge to our solution. Engagement without alignment is noise. Resonance is strategy.

---

### **Audience Pain Points (Listen First)**

Common frustrations to map against:

* **Confusion:** “I don’t get how this works.”

* **Inefficiency:** “This tool feels slow / messy.”

* **Lack of guidance:** “I don’t know what step to take next.”

* **Security:** “Will this protect my data/accounts?”

**Instruction:** Pull 3 recurring pain points per week from comments, surveys, DMs, or FAQs. Build content directly around them.

---

### **Content Pillars (Engagement Modes)**

**1\. Problem → Solution**  
 Show the frustration → Show our fix.  
 *Formats:* Reels with before/after, tutorials, carousels.

**2\. Educational Empowerment**  
 Teach something simple that makes their life easier.  
 *Formats:* How-to clips, carousel tips, blog breakdowns.

**3\. Testimonial / Case Study**  
 Let real people prove the promise.  
 *Formats:* 20-sec video quotes, screenshot posts, customer journeys.

**4\. Interactive Sparks**  
 Create back-and-forth trust.  
 *Formats:* Polls, quizzes, AMA stories, Q\&A reels, chatbot snippets.

---

### **Strategy for Engaging Content**

* **Hook fast:** First 3 seconds \= pattern interrupt (question, bold claim, visual surprise).

* **Story arc:** Pain → empathy → hope → solution.

* **Emotional resonance:** Use words that mirror feelings, not features.

* **CTA clarity:** Always close with one simple action (comment, click, share).

* **Relevance rhythm:** Post 3x/week minimum; mix 1 education, 1 problem/solution, 1 engagement (poll/story).

* **Trend riding:** Adapt trending reel formats, but anchor them in our message.

---

### **Workflow (Brand-Aligned Execution)**

**Plan:**

* Identify 3 core pain points \+ 1 trending theme.

* Map each pain point to a brand pillar (truth, design, resonance, content as legacy).

**Create:**

* Draft 3 posts: 1 Problem-Solution reel, 1 Educational carousel, 1 Proof/Interactive (testimonial/poll/AMA).

* Ensure all copy reflects Maverick’s tone: empathetic, poetic, resonant.

**Publish:**

* Schedule across IG, site blog, and newsletter.

* Stagger posts so each feels fresh (not cross-post spam).

**Engage (Daily):**

* Mirror user language in replies.

* Ask questions back to deepen dialogue.

* Redirect to resources (site, chatbot, Tier 1–2 products) when natural.

**Analyze:**

* Pull metrics (see below).

* Record what sparked the most resonance, not just reach.

* Adapt next week’s content accordingly.

---

### **Key Metrics (Resonance \+ Accuracy)**

* **Engagement Depth:** Saves, shares, meaningful comments (not just likes).

* **Click-to-Content Fit:** % of clicks leading to meaningful time-on-page.

* **Conversion Resonance:** % of content-driven traffic converting (free → paid, follower → subscriber).

* **Tone Alignment Feedback:** Comments like “This really speaks to me,” “This helped,” “Finally makes sense.”

* **Content Legacy Metric:** Did the post feel timeless enough to repurpose (yes/no each week)?

---

### **Function of This Layer**

This section translates prompts (Canvas C) into a repeatable **weekly production rhythm**. It equips Rochelle and the team to:

* Build directly from audience pain points.

* Mix formats consistently (education, problem/solution, interactive).

* Track resonance, not just reach.

* Keep Maverick’s mythic tone alive in every execution.
